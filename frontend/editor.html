Privacy Policy — SyntaxPDF

15-Point Zero-Retention & Data Protection Commitment

1. Zero Permanent File Storage: SyntaxPDF does not permanently store, archive, or back up any PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, or image files uploaded by users.

2. Automatic Post-Download Deletion: Server-processed files are placed in isolated temporary directories and are automatically and permanently wiped via SelfCleaningFileResponse immediately after the download stream closes.

3. Client-Side Browser Processing: Operations such as Merge PDF, Split PDF, Multi-Image to PDF, and Vector Text Editing execute directly inside your browser memory using WebAssembly/JavaScript whenever possible, meaning your files never even leave your device.

4. No Human Access to Documents: No developer, administrator, or third party can view, read, copy, or inspect the contents of your uploaded documents.

5. Password & Encryption Confidentiality: Passwords entered in Protect PDF or Unlock PDF are never logged, echoed, or saved in plain text or hashes on our servers.

6. Guest Usage Tracking (Local Storage): To provide 3 free document conversions without registration, SyntaxPDF stores a simple anonymous counter (`syntaxpdf_free_count`) in your browser's `localStorage`.

7. Authentication via Clerk: When you sign up or log in after your 3 free documents, authentication is securely handled by Clerk. We only receive basic profile identifiers (such as your email address) to verify your session.

8. Encrypted Data Transmission: All communications between your browser and SyntaxPDF servers are protected via HTTPS/TLS encryption.

9. Sandbox Subprocess Execution: Tools utilizing Ghostscript, LibreOffice, or Tesseract OCR run in isolated, non-shell sandboxes with `-dSAFER` flags to prevent cross-file access.

10. No Sale of Personal Information: We never sell, rent, trade, or monetize user emails, metadata, or document usage behavior with advertisers or data brokers.

11. Minimal Error Logging: System logs only record technical error codes (such as timeouts or corrupted headers) without storing filenames or sensitive document content.

12. Third-Party Service Boundaries: Aside from Clerk (for authentication) and Google TTS (when generating speech audio), no external APIs receive data from your interaction with SyntaxPDF.

13. User Right to Account Deletion: Registered users can manage or delete their account profile at any time directly through the Clerk user profile button in the navigation bar.

14. Children's Privacy: SyntaxPDF is a general-audience productivity utility and does not knowingly collect sensitive personal data from children under 13.

15. Direct Privacy Contact: For any privacy questions, data inquiries, or security reports, contact us at umarasifmirza439@gmail.com or mrtach777@gmail.com.
